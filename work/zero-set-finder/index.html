Black-box zero-set sampler

Independent research project | May to June 2026

Given a function you can only evaluate point by point, find the surface where it equals zero. Three versions: Sobol seeding with bisection, a gradient-rescue variant, and an active-learning Gaussian-process surrogate that picks the next query by the U-score |mu|/sigma and validates every returned point on the real function.

11x to 92xfewer function evaluations than the sampling baseline, n = 2 to 6
1e-8boundary residual |F| in all dimensions

What I did

  • Implemented all three samplers plus a shared bisection and Sobol toolkit in about 200 lines each.
  • Built the benchmark and figure scripts, pytest suites and the write-up with the derivations.
  • Documented the limits: smoothness assumption of the Matern kernel and the cubic GP cost above a few thousand points.

Tools: Python, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, matplotlib, pytest
