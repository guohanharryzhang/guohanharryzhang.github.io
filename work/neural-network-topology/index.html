Neural-network decision-boundary topology

Comillas Pontifical University (ICAI), Madrid | May to July 2026

A team study of the topology of decision boundaries learned by small ReLU networks. Networks are trained on known geometries, their boundaries are extracted, and persistent homology summarizes the result.

1,000random 2-8-1 ReLU networks in the stored experiment
892with stable detected boundaries; 736 had one component, 154 two, 2 three

What I did

  • Developed the adaptive boundary search using Sobol sampling, Gaussian-process uncertainty and bisection.
  • Contributed to PyTorch training with seeded, checkpointed and resumable experiments, and to GUDHI persistent-homology analysis.
  • Resolution checks do not prove exact topology; results depend on domain, sampling resolution and filtration scale. The team repository is private.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, GUDHI, NumPy
