vasp-flow: DFT workflow CLI for an HPC cluster

Luo Research Lab | March to October 2026

Running density-functional-theory calculations by hand meant juggling structure files, four VASP inputs, a Slurm script and SSH. vasp-flow walks through those steps, explains each one, and handles upload, submission and status checks.

What I did

  • Materials Project search and structure fetch, POSCAR/KPOINTS/INCAR generation from presets, POTCAR assembly on the cluster.
  • SSH and SCP transport with sbatch submission and squeue parsing; resumable per-job progress.
  • Used for the zinc surface-energy and adsorption study across three facets.

Tools: Python, pymatgen, paramiko, Slurm, VASP
