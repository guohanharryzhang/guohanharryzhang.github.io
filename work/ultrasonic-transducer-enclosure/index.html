Ultrasonic transducer and driver-board enclosure

Luo Research Lab | January 2026

The ultrasonic transducer and its driver board sat loose on the bench. I designed an enclosure that holds the board, routes the wiring and supports the transducer at the working position, and presented the design to the lab.

What I did

  • Modeled the enclosure in Autodesk Fusion with a driver-board cavity, wire openings and a transducer support.
  • Produced assembly and cutaway views for the lab review in January 2026.
  • Not yet fabricated; printing and fit checks are the next step.

Tools: Autodesk Fusion, 3D printing
