CanSat competition payload

AstroJays / SEDS JHU | Team contributor

A soda-can-sized payload for the CanSat competition. I worked on the flight-computer board, the sensors and the firmware.

What I did

  • Integrated and calibrated accelerometer, barometer and GPS measurements for descent and impact monitoring.
  • Wrote C/C++ firmware for sensor-data processing, flight-event logging and RF telemetry.
  • The flight computer is the team's Minmus board: an ESP32-WROOM-32 with a BMI088 IMU, ADXL377 high-g accelerometer, BMP390 barometer, SD card slot and USB-C programming. The board is an AstroJays team design that I worked on, alongside the firmware and sensor integration.

Tools: C, C++, ESP32, BMI088, ADXL377, BMP390, PCB, RF telemetry, sensor calibration
