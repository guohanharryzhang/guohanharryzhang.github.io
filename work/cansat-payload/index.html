CanSat competition payload

AstroJays / SEDS JHU | Team contributor

A soda-can-sized payload for the CanSat competition. I worked on the sensor and firmware side of the team.

What I did

  • Integrated and calibrated accelerometer, barometer and GPS measurements for descent and impact monitoring.
  • Wrote C/C++ firmware for sensor-data processing, flight-event logging and RF telemetry.

Tools: C, C++, microcontrollers, RF telemetry, sensor calibration
