Zinc electrodeposition automation

Luo Research Lab, Johns Hopkins | Spring 2025 to present

Professor Yuting Luo's group studies zinc-ion batteries. The deposition experiments were slow because every trial needed a person to handle additives, run the pumps and swap electrodes. I helped turn that into an automated workflow.

300%higher synthesis throughput
50+experimental trials on the automated setup
80%shorter hardware-iteration lead time

What I did

  • Integrated Opentrons OT-2 robotics with ultrasonic actuation so deposition cycles run without manual handling.
  • Wrote the Python/NI DAQ routines for ultrasonic waveform generation and pressure-valve sequencing used across 50+ trials.
  • Designed and 3D-printed the experimental fixtures and housings in Autodesk Fusion, which is where the 80% lead-time cut came from.
  • Helped automate the XRD characterization workflow and ran VASP surface-energy and adsorption calculations for three zinc facets on the Rockfish cluster.

Tools: Python, NI DAQ, Opentrons OT-2 and API, Autodesk Fusion, 3D printing, LabVIEW, Arduino, VASP, Slurm
